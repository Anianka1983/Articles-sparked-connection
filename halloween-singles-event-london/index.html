The Soundtrack, and an Optional Tarot Reading

Music matters more than most hosts admit. A playlist that is too loud or too niche pushes people into small huddles. The best of the 80s and 90s is one of the few soundtracks that suits almost every taste and every age in the room. People hum along, trade a smile over a favourite chorus, and suddenly there is something easy to talk about.

For guests who want something a little different, there is an optional tarot reading, available as a £10 extra with limited slots. It is entirely up to you. For some people it is simply a fun, slightly mysterious way to break the ice, and for others it is a good excuse to wander over and compare notes afterwards.

The Dress Code

What to Wear to a Masked Singles Evening

The one firm rule is an eye mask. Beyond that, glamour is encouraged and a full costume is entirely optional, so you can dress as elaborately or as simply as you like. Evening wear, a cocktail dress, a sharp jacket or a velvet blazer all look wonderful with a statement mask. If you do not own a mask, they are available to buy on the night for a small fee.

Choose something you can relax in. If you are tugging at your outfit all evening you will not be thinking about the person you are talking to. Comfortable shoes, a simple palette and one standout detail, such as a beautiful mask, earrings or a pocket square, are usually all you need.

A mask gives you a reason to start talking. There is nothing more natural than being curious about who is behind the mask opposite.
On masks, mystery and first conversations
The Psychology

Why a Mask Makes It Easier to Meet Someone

There is a psychological reason masked events feel so liberating. Psychologists describe an effect called online disinhibition: when people feel slightly anonymous, they become more open and more willing to say what they would normally keep to themselves. A mask creates a gentle, real-world version of this. You feel a little less watched and a little less judged, so approaching someone feels less daunting.

The mystery adds to it. It lowers the stakes of the first sentence, adds a spark of excitement, and often makes people braver than they expected to be. By the end of the evening, most guests say the mask did the nervous part for them.
